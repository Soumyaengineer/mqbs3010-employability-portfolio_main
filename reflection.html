Reflection and growth mindset

I am using feedback and self-awareness to become more workplace-ready.

My reflection is not only about what I completed. It is about what I noticed, what I changed and what I still need to improve.

Self-reflection

I learned that my technical work becomes stronger when I explain the business purpose behind it. A dashboard is more valuable when it answers a stakeholder question and supports a decision.

Self-regulation

I identified gaps in concise communication, confidence and role-specific evidence. I responded by narrowing the portfolio around one job role and choosing evidence that directly supports it.

Self-evaluation

I now evaluate my work by asking: is the evidence specific, is the recommendation clear and would a non-technical stakeholder understand why it matters?

Feedback to action

My development plan is specific to the CommSec role.

Challenge: Avoid generic statements.Action: I rewrote the portfolio around my target role, evidence and industry insights.
Challenge: Show more than technical skills.Action: I placed 2030 skills at the centre of the portfolio and used technical evidence as support.
Challenge: Explain why evidence matters.Action: I added captions that connect each image to employability and workplace value.
Challenge: Keep improving after assessment.Action: I will build a customer analytics project and practise short insight presentations.
