My main 2030 skill focus

My strongest development priority is becoming the type of analyst who can question data, communicate uncertainty, listen to stakeholders and turn analysis into action.

Where technical skills fit

SQL, Power BI, Excel and Python are important, but I treat them as tools. The workplace value comes from how I use them to support reliable decisions and practical recommendations.
