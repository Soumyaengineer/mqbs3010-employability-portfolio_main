MQBS3010 industry guest insights

I connected industry advice to the analyst I am trying to become.

These insights helped me move beyond a technical view of employability. Future analysts need evidence, scepticism, stakeholder awareness, resilience and continuous learning.

Fractional Chief Data and AI Officer / Board Director
“You either have data or just an opinion.”

This insight connects directly to business analytics because decisions should be supported by evidence rather than assumption. I also took away the need for professional scepticism with AI: I should question outputs, sense-check models and apply critical thinking before recommending action.

Connection to role: data quality, evidence-based decisions and responsible AI use.
Change and Transformation Consultant
Data creates value only when people understand, trust and adopt it.

This insight helped me see that data recommendations can fail if stakeholders are not supported through change. For a Data Management Analyst role, I need resilience, curiosity and communication skills so that insights can become practical action.

Connection to role: stakeholder engagement, frontline adoption and change communication.
Executive Dean, Macquarie Business School
Change is constant, and data supports better decisions during uncertainty.

This insight reinforced that analysts should not only report what happened. I need to explore options, remain open to alternative pathways and communicate clearly when there is no single perfect answer.

Connection to role: adaptability, options thinking and continuous learning.
